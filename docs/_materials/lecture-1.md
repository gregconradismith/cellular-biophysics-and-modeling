# Biophysics, dynamics, neuron doctrines, and organization of the brain 

## Required Reading

Note: For readings that are behind a paywall, see the password protected course page titled [**Readings**](../readings/).

1.  [**Apologia and Introduction** of *Cellular Biophysics and Modeling: A primer on the systems biology of excitable cells*.](https://doi.org/10.1017/9780511793905)  (15 pages)
2.  **[The organization of the brain.](https://doi.org/10.1038/scientificamerican0979-88)** Nauta WJ, Feirtag M. *Sci Am.* 1979 Sep; 241(3):88-111. (Read the first half, pp. 17-25)
3.  <a href="https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1114394" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Neuroscience. The neuron doctrine, redux.</strong></a> Bullock TH, Bennett MV, Johnston D, Josephson R, Marder E, Fields RD. Science. 2005 Nov 4;310(5749):791-3.
