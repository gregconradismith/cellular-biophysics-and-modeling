# Post-Synaptic Potentials, Qualitative Analysis, Characteristic Times

## Resources (Optional Reading)

- There is no required reading for this lecture.  However, the lecture is based on <strong>Function families and characteristic times</strong>, as presented in Chapter 5 of <em>Cellular Biophysics and Modeling: A Primer on the Systems Biology of Excitable Cells</em>. You may wish to refer to Chapter 5 for additional help and a deeper understanding. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1017/9780511793905">DOI</a>
- This <strong>MIT Mathlet on graph features</strong> may prove helpful if you are having difficulty with the qualitative analysis of functions. <a href="http://mathlets.org/mathlets/graph-features/">Mathlet</a>
- The free graphing software <strong>Desmos</strong> is highly recommended. <a href="https://www.desmos.com">Software</a>
