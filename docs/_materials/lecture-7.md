# Voltage-dependent currents and bistability

## Required Reading

1.  **Voltage-Gated Ionic Currents.** Chapter 10 of *Cellular Biophysics and Modeling: A primer on the systems biology of excitable cells.* [\[DOI\]](https://doi.org/10.1017/9780511793905)
2.  **Regenerative Ionic Currents and Bistability.** Chapter 11 of *Cellular Biophysics and Modeling: A primer on the systems biology of excitable cells.* [\[DOI\]](https://doi.org/10.1017/9780511793905)
3.  Sigworth FJ. **Structural biology: Life's transistors.** *Nature.* 2003 May 1;423(6935):21-2. [\[PubMed\]](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12721605/)
4.  Dolphin AC. **A short history of voltage-gated calcium channels.** *Br J Pharmacol.* 2006 Jan;147 Suppl 1(Suppl 1):S56-62. [\[PubMed\]](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16402121/)
5.  Lipscombe D, Helton TD, Xu W. **L-type calcium channels: the low down.** *J Neurophysiol.* 2004 Nov;92(5):2633-41. [\[PubMed\]](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15486420/)
6.  Kiehn O, Eken T. **Functional role of plateau potentials in vertebrate motor neurons.** *Curr Opin Neurobiol.* 1998 Dec;8(6):746-52. [\[PubMed\]](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9914232/)
7.  Scholarpedia entry for **Up and down states** by Charles Wilson. [\[Article\]](http://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Up_and_down_states)
