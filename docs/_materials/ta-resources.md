Office hours are a great opportunity to ask questions, go over tricky concepts, or just get some extra practice with the material. My office hours will be held <strong>Tuesdays and Thursday, from 6 pm to 7 pm in ISC 0280</strong>. You’re welcome to drop by with anything you’d like to review.

If you’d rather not ask on the spot—or if you think of a question ahead of time—you can also drop it in <strong>PollEverywhere (PollEv)</strong> using the box right here on the page. Submissions are <strong>anonymous</strong>, so you can ask whatever’s on your mind without worrying. I’ll pull those up during office hours so we can talk through them together.

And just a reminder: <strong>no question is too small or too basic</strong>. Chances are, if you’re wondering about something, other people are too—so asking helps everyone.

✨ Hope to see you there!
