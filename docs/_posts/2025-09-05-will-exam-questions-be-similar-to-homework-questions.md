Student: Are the problem set questions representative of what will be on the exam?

Professor: Not necessarily. The problem sets are <strong>exercises</strong> designed to <strong>help you learn</strong> the material. A homework question could require 20-30 minutes to work through. That would not be an appropriate question for a timed in-class exam.

Most students should expect homework to take several hours. This assumes you have attended lecture and read the required readings carefully. The CMB Primer has many worked examples; often these are similar to the homework questions.

The example below gives a sense of the format and level of work expected on a Unit 1 exam. Some questions require a minute of working through things on scratch paper.
