Attached is an example Exam 1 from Fall 2024, courtesy Prof. Christopher Del Negro. An answer key will not be distributed, but some of the questions can be used for study and practice.

Questions 4, 6, and 17-19, are not representative of the exam that I am writing (e.g., we have not yet discussed negative feedback, we are not using Berkeley Madonna).

Questions 17-19 are doable if you understand how to derive ODEs from chemical reaction schemes using mass action kinetics (discussed in Chapter 4 of the textbook). However, Chapter 4 was not required reading, so these questions are not representative of the exam I am writing.

Questions 27-30 can be done without Berkeley Madonna and the concepts here are important.
