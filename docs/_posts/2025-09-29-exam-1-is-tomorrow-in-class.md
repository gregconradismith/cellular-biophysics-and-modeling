Student: I hope you are doing well. I am confused about how to estimate the threshold voltage without an explicit voltage-dependent conductance g(V) curve for question 33 on the Fall 2015 exam. Can you provide any clarification?

Professor: When the current is at its maximum, all channels are open, and the slope of the current vs. voltage curve will be constant, so you get a line. Trace that line back until the actual current is half what it would be if all channels were open.
