Student: I was wondering what sign of β (the trace of the constant coefficient matrix A) is used when calculating eigenvalues. On page 276 of the textbook, the sign of β is positive, but in the lecture slides posted to the class blog, the sign of β is negative. Thanks for your help!

Professor: Good catch. The sign of β is positive in the quadratic formula (16.5) because it occurs with a negative sign in the quadratic expression for lambda (16.4). There is a typo on the lecture slide (on the right).  

—Greg
